Campus Pages Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Campus Pages account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email to:

devforgelabs.review@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Data Deleted

  • User profile information
  • Uploaded notes and resources
  • Bookmarks and preferences
  • Associated account information

Data Retention

Certain records may be retained for up to 30 days for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance purposes.
